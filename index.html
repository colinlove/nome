How Device Sync Works

ClockSync Metronome anchors its beat grid directly to the international wall-clock time (device time) rather than the instant you tap "Play".

The Core Rules:

  • Integer BPM: At $N$ BPM, exactly $N$ beats fit in each 60-second minute.
  • Downbeat at :00.000: Every minute starts with Beat 1 (Downbeat).
  • No Arbitrary Start: Starting playback immediately jumps into whatever beat and phase matches the current wall clock second.

Multi-Device Synchronization:

Because smartphones, laptops, and smart devices synchronize with NTP (Network Time Protocol) or GPS time servers, two or more separate devices opening this webpage will play clicks and flash visual cues in lockstep without needing any local Wi-Fi pairing or Bluetooth.

Visual Cues:

  • Strobe: Full screen flash on every beat with a rapid fade, with stronger accents on beat 1. Switchable dark/light themes.
  • Bouncing Ball: Realistic gravity bounce, ideal for faster tempos (>= 100 BPM) to track timing physically.
  • Circular Progress: Radial sweep and segment countdown for slower tempos (< 100 BPM).
